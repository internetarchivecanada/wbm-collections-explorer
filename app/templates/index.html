Notes on the numbers

What a "document" is

Each collection is its own index, and a hit means different things in each: a captured web page, a PDF, a video, a social post. The unit is named on every tile. Counts are index sizes reported by the Wayback Machine, not counts of distinct URLs.

Two collections have no profile

PDFs — Web-Wide and Telegram Public Channels are large enough that a whole-collection aggregation times out server-side, so they show counts only. Everything else carries a full profile.
